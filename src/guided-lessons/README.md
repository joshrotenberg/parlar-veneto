# Guided Lessons

These short lessons are for learners who know some Italian but do not yet
understand Venetian. Italian is the bridge; English carries the explanation.
The goal is to use a small amount of Venetian successfully before adding more.

Each lesson takes about fifteen minutes:

1. Read the dialogue with all three languages visible.
2. Read it again while covering the English column.
3. Study the three or four patterns under **Notice**.
4. Complete the exercises aloud as well as in writing.
5. Check the answer key only after making an attempt.

The unmarked forms follow the central Padovano-Vicentino base used elsewhere in
the book. A regional note at the end of each lesson prepares you for forms you
may hear from other speakers.

## The First Three Lessons

- [Lesson 1: Meeting Someone](lesson01-meeting.md) — names, origins, and *Come
  steto?*
- [Lesson 2: At the Bacaro](lesson02-bacaro.md) — ordering, hunger, thirst, and
  numbers
- [Lesson 3: Talking About Family](lesson03-family.md) — relatives, possession,
  age, and third-person clitics

These lessons are a beginner path through the larger reference grammar. When a
pattern interests you, follow the chapter links—but you do not need to master
the linked chapter before continuing.
